# 2D art quality gates

## Shared

- Canvas dimensions, transparency, color space intent, target, and deliverable are explicit.
- The selected Style Contract is accepted by the author.
- Composition, draft, revision, validation, and export remain separate checkpoints.
- Reference rights status remains attached to every reference.

## Pixel

- Integer canvas and scaling; no blurred resampling.
- Palette count stays within the contract.
- Clusters, outlines, isolated pixels, frame bounds, pivots, and atlas slicing are checked.

## Anime and custom raster

- Line, shape language, proportion, palette, lighting, and shading follow accepted invariants.
- Character identity and required details remain consistent across revisions.
- Visual approval remains an author decision, not a deterministic validator claim.
